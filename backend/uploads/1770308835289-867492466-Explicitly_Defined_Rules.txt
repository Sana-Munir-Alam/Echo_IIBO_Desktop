🧠 The Channel Speaks

This is no ordinary text.
Some symbols are echoes of the channel itself, meant to guide the transmission, not convey information.
These letters appear at boundaries and repeat without forming words. They are routing echoes — ignore them.

Other symbols scream like static — loud, meaningless interference.
Only by silencing this static can you hear the message clearly.

Once the noise subsides, numbers emerge.
They are the voices of the message itself, speaking in the oldest cipher known:
1 = A, 2 = B, … 26 = Z.

But the channel leaves traces of echoes.
Triangles (∆) hover near numbers like shadows, adding secret vowels:

Odd numbers whisper E

Even numbers hum O

Only the first triangle before a number counts; extra triangles are just echoes repeating in the void.

Then there are Sigmas (Σ).
They never speak alone.
Instead, they borrow the tone of the nearest number to their left on the same line.
Divide that number by 4 and let the remainder reveal the consonant they imitate:

Remainder	Σ becomes
1			S
2			T
3			R
0			N

Assemble letters, vowels, and borrowed consonants carefully — but beware.
The channel has pushed the message forward by two letters, like a code within a code.
To hear it properly, you must step back — shift the entire message back by 2 letters.
